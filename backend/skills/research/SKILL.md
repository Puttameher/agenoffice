# Research Skill

**Category**: Information Gathering  
**Agent**: Nova (Research Agent)

## Description
Deep research skill for gathering structured, up-to-date information from web sources, synthesizing findings, and producing well-cited reports.

## Capabilities
- Web search via `browser` tool
- Source credibility evaluation
- Structured report generation (executive summary, key findings, citations)
- Competitive intelligence and market research
- Academic literature synthesis

## Usage
Assign tasks that require:
- Market research and trend analysis
- Product comparison reports  
- Background research on topics, companies, or technologies
- News summarization and briefings

## Example Tasks
- "Research the latest trends in autonomous AI agents"
- "Summarize recent news about OpenAI and Anthropic"
- "Create a competitive analysis of CRM platforms"

## Tools Required
- `browser`
