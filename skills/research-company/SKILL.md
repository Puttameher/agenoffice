# research-company

**Category**: Market Intelligence  
**Agent**: Nova  

## Description
Conducts competitive research and synthesizes market profile reports.

## Capabilities
- Systematic task automation
- Self-verifying procedure

## Procedure
1. Navigate to company website and about pages.
2. Extract executive leadership, core product offerings, and recent press releases.
3. Read internal knowledge notes to benchmark against known industry metrics.
4. Synthesize research brief and publish to Obsidian vault.
